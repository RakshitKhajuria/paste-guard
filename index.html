Don't paste your secrets into AI chats.

Paste Guard checks what you paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and more. If it finds an email, API key, card number, Aadhaar, PAN or UPI ID, it offers to paste a masked version instead. Everything happens on your device.

Free. No account. No tracking. Nothing is uploaded: you can check that in your browser.

What you get

  • It catches what people forget

    Emails, phone numbers, cards, Aadhaar, PAN, UPI IDs, IFSC, GSTIN, IBAN, API keys from many providers, private keys and passwords in connection strings. See the full list.

  • You stay in control

    Paste masked, paste anyway, or cancel. Press Ignore on a value and it is never flagged again.

  • Private by design

    No network requests, no analytics, no remote code, no account. On the sites listed below it checks what you paste and, with Restore on, reads the page text to show your original values again in replies. Nothing leaves your device.

  • Your values come back in replies

    The AI only sees placeholders like [EMAIL_1]. When it answers using them, your browser shows your real values again, on your device. Your message box keeps the placeholders, and Alt+R (Option+R on a Mac) switches the view. You can turn this off in the popup.

  • Mask your own words

    Select a client or project name in the message box and press Alt+Shift+M (Option+Shift+M on a Mac). It is masked now and in every future paste, using a personal dictionary kept on your device (up to 10 words, free).

Verify it yourself

You should not have to take our word for it. These checks take about five minutes.

  1. Permissions

    Open chrome://extensions, click Details on Paste Guard. It asks only to read and change data on the AI sites listed below. Its only API permission is storage, for your settings.

  2. No network

    On chrome://extensions turn on Developer mode, click Inspect views: service worker and open the Network tab. Then right-click the toolbar icon and choose Inspect popup, open its Network tab and change a setting. Both stay empty.

  3. Watch a paste

    On a supported site, open DevTools, go to Network, and paste a fake key such as AKIA plus 16 capital letters. The warning appears and no request is made by the paste.

  4. Read what is stored

    In the popup's DevTools console run chrome.storage.local.get(null, console.log). You will see settings, a counter and, if you used Ignore, one-way hashes. Never pasted text.

  5. Check Restore

    With Restore on, mask a fake value, then in the popup's DevTools console run chrome.storage.session.get(null, console.log): you will see a restore: entry for the site, held in memory. Run chrome.storage.local.get(null, console.log): the original value is not there. Keep the Network tab open while a reply is restored: no request is made.

  6. Inspect the installed files

    The extension's JavaScript is delivered to your browser and can be inspected in the installed package. Open chrome://extensions, click Details on Paste Guard, then open DevTools on its popup or service worker (Inspect popup, or Inspect views with Developer mode on) and browse its files in the Sources tab. This site does not publish the source code.
