Interactive tour

Do it yourself on a stand-in chat page: paste something sensitive, choose what happens, see your values come back in the reply, mask a word of your own, and change a setting.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. The warning and the detectors are the real ones, with made-up data only. Your own clipboard is never read.

About this demo. This is a simulation on a stand-in page, not a real AI product, and the reply is made up. The real behaviour happens on the supported sites (8 AI chat sites). The extension is a safety net, not a guarantee.

You have seen it all

  • It checks what you paste and offers to mask it first, with 14 detector switches you control.
  • The AI only receives placeholders; your screen shows your values again in the reply.
  • Words you mask yourself are kept in a personal dictionary on your device.
  • It works on 8 AI chat sites and does nothing anywhere else.

Safety net, not a guarantee. It finds common patterns and can miss unusual formats.
